# Example requirements document (test fixture, NOT a real regulation)

This document exists so the starter rule pack has a written source for every
threshold while the team waits for the real regulation (see Defaults, item 5 of
the build plan). Replace it with the uploaded requirement document; the
Interpreter (I3–I5) turns that document into an approved pack.

Art. 1: Exits. Every dwelling has at least one exit door to the outside.

Art. 2: Circulation. Every habitable room (bedroom, living room, kitchen) reaches
an exit without passing through a bathroom, a WC or a bedroom other than itself.

Art. 2.1: Access. Every enclosed room has at least one door or opening.

Art. 3: Bedrooms. A bedroom is not reached only through another bedroom.

Art. 4: Garage. No door or opening connects a garage directly to a bedroom.

Art. 5: Dead ends. A habitable room with a single door has at least one window.

Art. 6.1: Door width. Every door has a clear width of at least 0.80 m.

Art. 6.2: Corridor width. Corridors have a clear width of at least 1.00 m along their whole length.

Art. 6.3: Bedroom size. A bedroom has a floor area of at least 9.00 m² and a minimum width of at least 2.20 m.

Art. 6.4: Living room size. A living room has a floor area of at least 14.00 m².

Art. 6.5: Kitchen size. A kitchen has a floor area of at least 7.00 m².

Art. 7: Light. In habitable rooms the glazed area is at least 1/6 of the floor area.

Art. 7.2: Escape window. Every bedroom has an exterior window at least 0.60 m wide and 1.00 m high.

Art. 8: Travel distance. From any habitable room, the travel distance to an exit is at most 15 m.

Art. 9: Drawing quality.
 a) every enclosed room carries a label;
 b) every plan sheet carries dimensions;
 c) dimension chains add up to their overall dimension;
 d) written dimensions match the drawn geometry;
 e) stated room areas match the drawn rooms within 5 %;
 f) door and window tags match the schedule (same widths), every tag has a schedule row and every row is used;
 g) the title block states the scale, the revision and the date;
 h) door swings do not collide;
 i) every door and window carries a tag.
