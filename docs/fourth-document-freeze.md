# Fourth document label freeze

The fourth document is the seismic regulation RPS 2000, version 2011,
annexed to Decree 2-12-682, in the French official translation of the
*Bulletin Officiel* no. 6206, 21 November 2013. The PDF is an official
gazette reproduction hosted by the government urban agency in Tétouan.
This fixed edition is used as a research source.

The [source file](../reports/rps-fourth-source.json) contains 17 obligations
with document context. The separate [labels](../reports/rps-fourth-labels.json)
contain manual interpretations, expected applicability and exception facts,
and 48 synthetic project scenarios. They were authored without invoking a
checker or drafting model on the fourth document.

The cases cover building class, faults, risk sites, foundation systems,
rock-anchorage exemptions, pile restraint, unusual plan forms, adjacent
blocks, joint dimensions, material-dependent defaults, structural
interaction and analysis-method conditions. One clause's ambiguous height
and mass wording is explicitly labelled for review. The printed regularity
cross-reference is preserved as a limitation.

For future drafting, provide the source file's clause and source-context
fields only. Do not supply gold interpretations, project scope, scenario
facts, labels or applicability condition lists to the drafter. Measure
condition extraction against the hidden labels, and ask for project evidence
when a needed condition is unknown.

This is the only fresh held-out document in the current round. All 46
earlier obligations are development data. No model or checker has run on
these 17 obligations. No tuning may use their outcomes.

The benchmark author wrote all labels without independent review. The
project scenarios are small and synthetic. Any future ledger attestations
in those scenarios must remain labelled synthetic. The PDF SHA-256 is
`7650732566d1dadd3a17d9c2ba7112cbe609ce869e77beb80bd2c5b21f3dc714`.
