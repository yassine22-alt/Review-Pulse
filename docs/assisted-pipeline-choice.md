# Preregistered assisted-pipeline selection

Registered before the new deterministic-extraction evaluation. The fourth document stays sealed.

A candidate must reach the intended rule with at most one semantic edit on at least 50% of the frozen numeric development clauses, including unsupported clauses in the denominator. It must have zero source-wrong or incomplete approvable candidates among at least ten source-reviewed approvable drafts. Scope, entity ownership, units, exceptions and quantifiers are part of correctness; a matching threshold is insufficient. Ten blinded independent grades must be returned and their agreement reported before selection.

Among qualifying candidates, choose lower median semantic edits, then fewer rewrites, then fewer measured tokens per finished clause. Missing evidence is distinct from missing meaning. No candidate qualifies through abstention alone. If measurements are missing or no candidate qualifies, select none and keep the fourth document sealed. Never relax these criteria after observing results.

Freeze the selected code, model and prompts before the one held-out run. API unavailability is separate from semantic failure; resuming an identical uncompleted cached request is allowed. No changed-prompt rerun after a semantic output.

Machine-readable criteria: [registration](../reports/assisted-pipeline-preregistration.json). All existing grades are assistant/benchmark-author grades, not independent human review.
