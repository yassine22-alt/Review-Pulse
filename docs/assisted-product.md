# ReviewPulse: assisted requirement review

**An expert-assisted drafting and checking workspace, not a general regulation
interpreter.** Reviewers keep the original clause beside a proposed rule, correct
its meaning, approve it explicitly, and inspect what the plan evidence can settle.

**Supported drafting.** Explicit numeric table rows can supply quantities,
comparators, column units and typed applicability cells. The model selects a
fact name from a closed, dimension-compatible catalogue menu. Simple French and
English prose can supply one explicit comparator, quantity and unit. Explicit
ratios retain numerator and denominator; per-entity wording or implicit minima
are not guessed. Every decision retains source evidence. Unit, source-span,
round-trip, ownership and composition validators can veto a candidate. None of
these checks proves that the selected fact has the right meaning.

**Assisted work.** A reviewer inspects numeric fields, units and existing scope
conditions. Conditional, relational, qualitative or incomplete clauses enter a
formalization queue. Its advanced editor is intended for someone who understands
the finite rule language and building requirements; it is not a finished
nontechnical rule builder. The reviewer can formalize with validated fact
declarations, but a declaration creates no measured value. Source/context or
unsupported-computation gaps remain visible. Saving and approval are separate.

**Evidence and findings.** The local plan pipeline supplies detected spaces,
openings and measurements, with their uncertainty. An approved interpretation
can still be unresolved because scale, classification, inventory, connections,
schedules or specialist evidence are missing. An unlabelled room is not assumed
to be outside a rule; an incomplete universal inventory cannot establish a pass.
Nominal door sizes are not clear openings. No new plan computation was added in
this sprint, and detection counts do not certify complete inventories.

**Observed review effort.** Start/pause/save controls record original and edited
slots, diffs, input events, failed saves and server elapsed time. Completed human
sessions are reported separately for in-scope drafts and formalization. A root
replacement is a rewrite, not one low-burden semantic edit. Timings include
reading/idle time except explicit pauses; identity is self-reported. Real teammate
sessions have not yet been collected, so editing time and burden are unmeasured.

**Evidence today.** On the same 36 numeric development obligations, 12/36 are
fully correct with zero edits, 0/36 need one edit, 0/36 need multiple local edits,
21/36 need formalization and 3/36 need context. This is unchanged from baseline;
the catalogue is a fixture, not proof that a real floor plan supplies thermal
properties. Assistant source review finds 0/12 wrong approvable table rules.
All 28/28 labelled out-of-scope development clauses are queued, but so are 6/18
in-scope clauses. The fourth-document run queued 17/17 and retained none.

**Limits.** No general interpretation, held-out accuracy improvement or safety
claim. Labels lack independent review; historical ledger attestations are
synthetic. Capabilities are finite, samples small and the 25% scale bound
provisional. Real CAD generalization is unmeasured. Drawings stay local, provider
keys remain in environment variables, and no hosted billing is enabled.
